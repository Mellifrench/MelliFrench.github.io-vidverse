Vidverse Rules

By using Vidverse, you agree to follow these rules. Violations may result in content removal or account suspension.

  1. No NSFW: Do not upload or share not safe for work content, including any explicit sexual material, nudity, or suggestive themes that could be inappropriate in a professional or public setting.
  2. No NSFL: Do not upload or share not safe for life content, such as graphic violence, gore, disturbing imagery, or any material that could cause severe emotional distress or trauma to viewers.
  3. You must be over the age of 10 to use Vidverse: For safety and compliance reasons, users must be at least 11 years old; this helps ensure a family-friendly environment and protects younger children from potential online risks.
  4. Please be respectful: Treat all users with kindness and respect; avoid harassment, hate speech, bullying, discrimination, or any behavior that could harm or offend others in the community.
  5. No spam or advertising: Do not post repetitive content, unsolicited promotions, or irrelevant links designed to drive traffic or sales without genuine community value.
  6. No illegal content: Do not share or promote content that violates local, national, or international laws, including but not limited to threats, fraud, or encouragement of criminal activities.
  7. Respect intellectual property: Only upload videos that you own or have the rights to share; avoid infringing on copyrights, trademarks, or other intellectual property rights.
  8. No misinformation or deception: Do not spread false information, conspiracy theories, or misleading content that could harm individuals or society; always aim for accuracy and truthfulness.
  9. No impersonation: Do not pretend to be someone else, including celebrities, public figures, or other users, to deceive or mislead others.
  10. Report violations: If you see content that breaks these rules, use the report button to notify moderators; do not engage in vigilante actions.
